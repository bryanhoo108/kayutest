Known problems and how we handled them:
- Shah Alam Home Depot has a BLANK credit limit. Treated as "unknown", never as zero.
  It can never fire the over-limit rule; instead it gets its own LOW/Data card (R6).
- INV-261462, Port Dickson Home, is NEGATIVE: -RM 4,800, "Credit note CN-0231, returned
  dining set". It is netted into that dealer's balance and into the Current bucket.
  Summing the bucket without it overstates receivables by RM 4,800.
- INV-261416, Borneo Living KK, RM 187,400, 95 days, note "Disputes delivery damage on
  2 sofas". Per credit-terms.md a dispute is NOT chased but DOES count against the credit
  limit. So: excluded from the chase rules R1-R4, included in the R5 limit calculation,
  included in the "past 60 days" number, and given its own LOW/Dispute card (R7).
- Checked and clean: no total rows in any tab, one date format (YYYY-MM-DD / YYYY-MM),
  one currency (RM), CTR is a percent (0.72 to 3.13) not a fraction.
- Last contact is blank for every Current-bucket row. Expected, not a defect.

THE QUEUE RULES
R1. Undisputed invoice more than 90 days past due -> HIGH, managing director. Director
    letter, consider credit insurance or legal. [credit-terms.md chase sequence]
R2. Undisputed invoice 61-90 days past due -> HIGH, finance manager + sales director.
    Meeting or call with the owner, payment plan if needed. [credit-terms.md]
R3. Undisputed invoice 31-60 days past due -> MEDIUM, finance manager. New shipments on
    hold, phone call then written notice. [credit-terms.md]
R4. Dealer with invoices 1-30 days past due -> LOW, accounts. ONE CARD PER DEALER, not per
    invoice, because a statement of account covers the account. 1-7 days is a statement,
    8-30 is a chase email. [credit-terms.md]
R5. Dealer balance over its credit limit -> HIGH, finance manager. Hold new orders until
    approved. Disputed invoices still count. [credit-terms.md terms]
R6. Credit limit blank in the sheet -> LOW/Data, finance manager. [our decision, move 1]
R7. Invoice noted as disputed -> LOW/Dispute, finance manager. A person calls, never a
    template. Excluded from R1-R4. [credit-terms.md, what stays human]
R8. A cost category more than 25% above its prior three-month average, in the last three
    months -> LOW/Costs, finance manager. [DEFAULT: no cost threshold exists in the
    documents. Change this if the business has a real one.]
R9. A revenue category up more than 25% over two months -> GOOD, sales director.
    [DEFAULT, same caveat.]

WHAT THE PAGE NEVER DOES
No fetch, no external script, no external font, no login, no send. Every button either
navigates inside the page, copies a prompt to the clipboard, or ticks a box that lives in
that browser's localStorage on that laptop. It opens with Wi-Fi off.
